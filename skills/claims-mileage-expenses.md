# Field Services — Claims & Mileage Reimbursement Policy

**Department:** Field Services

---

## Overview

This policy governs the reimbursement of claims and mileage expenses incurred by employees using their personal vehicles or making out-of-pocket expenditures for official business purposes.

---

## Types of Claims

| Claim Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Mileage** | Reimbursement for use of personal vehicle for official business travel |
| **Toll** | Toll charges incurred during official travel |
| **Parking** | Parking fees at client sites, offices, or between locations |
| **Lodging** | Hotel accommodation for overnight business trips |
| **Meals** | Meal allowance for business travel (overnight or extended day trips) |
| **Miscellaneous** | Petrol (if not included in mileage), office supplies, printing, courier (with receipt) |

---

## Mileage Rate

### Standard Rate

| Component | Rate |
|---|---|
| **Standard mileage rate** | **RM 0.60 per km** (or as set by company policy) |
| Review frequency | Periodically reviewed (at least annually) based on fuel costs, inflation, and market benchmarks |

### Rate Includes

The mileage rate is intended to cover:
- Fuel costs
- Vehicle wear and tear
- Maintenance and servicing
- Insurance proportionate to business use

### When Mileage Applies

- Travel from office to client site
- Travel between client sites
- Travel from home to client site (if direct travel is more efficient and pre-approved)
- Travel for business errands (bank, post office, supplies, etc.)

### When Mileage Does NOT Apply

- Regular commute from home to office (not reimbursable)
- Personal detours or side trips
- Personal vehicle used without prior approval

---

## Eligibility

| Condition | Eligible? |
|---|---|
| Employee uses personal vehicle for official business | Yes |
| Employee travels between office and client site | Yes |
| Employee uses company vehicle | No (fuel paid by company) |
| Employee uses public transport | Reimbursed per actual receipt |
| Employee travels for personal reasons during work hours | No |

---

## Claim Process

### Step-by-Step

```
Employee completes claim form (or HR system submission)
           ↓
Attaches supporting documents (receipts, log sheet)
           ↓
Submit to direct Supervisor for approval
           ↓
Manager reviews and endorses
           ↓
Finance Department reviews and processes
           ↓
Payment in next payroll cycle
```

### Required Information

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Employee name & ID | Full name and employee number |
| Claim date(s) | Date(s) of travel/expense |
| From location | Starting point |
| To location | Destination |
| Distance (km) | Total kilometres travelled |
| Purpose | Reason for travel (e.g., client visit, site inspection) |
| Receipts | Attached for toll, parking, lodging, meals, miscellaneous |

### Mileage Log Sheet Format

| Date | From | To | Distance (km) | Purpose | Toll/Parking (RM) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DD/MM/YYYY | Office | Client A | 12.5 | Site visit | 4.20 |
| DD/MM/YYYY | Client A | Client B | 8.3 | Meeting | 0.00 |
| DD/MM/YYYY | Client B | Office | 10.0 | Return | 3.50 |

### Digital Submission

- Claims can be submitted via the HR system / expense management platform.
- Digital receipts (scanned or photographed) are accepted.
- GPS log data may be used for automated mileage tracking (if enabled).

---

## Approval Chain

| Claim Amount (RM) | Approver(s) |
|---|---|
| ≤ RM 500 | Supervisor only |
| RM 500 – RM 2,000 | Supervisor → Manager |
| RM 2,000 – RM 5,000 | Supervisor → Manager → Finance Head |
| > RM 5,000 | Supervisor → Manager → Finance Head → Director / CEO |

### Emergency Approvals

- In the absence of the primary approver, the next-in-line or designated alternate may approve.
- Retrospective approval must be obtained within 5 working days.

---

## Reimbursement Timeline

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Employee submits claim | By the 3rd working day of the month (for prior month) |
| Supervisor/Manager approval | Within 5 working days |
| Finance review | Within 5 working days |
| Payment | Next payroll cycle (typically last working day of the month) |

**Urgent claims:** May be processed within 3 working days for amounts ≤ RM 500 (with Management approval).

## Tax Treatment

| Expense Type | Tax Treatment |
|---|---|
| Mileage allowance (≤ RM 0.60/km) | **Tax-exempt** (no need to report in EA form) |
| Mileage allowance (> RM 0.60/km) | Excess is taxable as perquisite |
| Toll / parking (actual with receipt) | **Tax-exempt** (reimbursement of actual cost) |
| Lodging (actual with receipt) | **Tax-exempt** (business-related) |
| Meal allowance (per diem or actual) | **Tax-exempt** (subject to IRB guidelines) |
| Miscellaneous (with receipt) | **Tax-exempt** (business-related) |

### IRB Guidelines

- Per diem meal rates must follow LHDN's gazetted rates (currently up to RM 50/day for domestic travel, varies for overseas).
- Claims exceeding reasonable amounts may be subject to tax inquiry.

## Documentation & Record Keeping

### Receipt Requirements

| Expense | Receipt Required? | Accepted Formats |
|---|---|---|
| Toll | Yes (Touch 'n Go statement or receipt) | Digital or physical |
| Parking | Yes | Digital or physical |
| Lodging | Yes (itemised hotel bill) | Digital or physical |
| Meals | Yes (restaurant receipt) | Digital or physical |
| Mileage | Log sheet (no receipt needed for mileage) | HR system record |
| Miscellaneous | Yes | Digital or physical |

**Receipts must include:**
- Merchant name
- Date
- Amount
- Itemised list of charges (for lodging)
- GST/SST registration number (if applicable)

### Retention Period

| Document | Retention |
|---|---|
| Claim forms & approvals | **6 years** (for audit and tax purposes) |
| Receipts (original or digital) | **6 years** |
| Mileage log sheets | **6 years** |
| GPS tracking data (if applicable) | **6 years** |

---

## Audit & Compliance

### Random Verification

- Finance or Internal Audit may randomly verify 10–20% of all claims each quarter.
- Cross-check mileage claims against GPS data, log sheets, or calendar appointments.
- Verify receipts against claim amounts.

### Fraudulent Claims

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Inflated mileage | Repayment of overclaimed amount + disciplinary warning |
| Falsified receipts | Summary dismissal (misconduct) |
| Claiming for personal trips | Repayment + disciplinary action |
| Repeated violations | Dismissal + potential legal action |

### Audit Trail

- All approvals must be recorded with date/time and approver name.
- HR system should maintain an immutable log of submissions, approvals, and payments.

---

## Special Cases

### Overseas Travel

- Mileage does not apply overseas (company arranges transport or rental car).
- Per diem rates follow LHDN overseas travel rates.
- Receipts must be in English or accompanied by a translation.

### Extended Field Assignments

- For assignments > 30 consecutive days, consider a fixed monthly travel allowance instead of per-trip claims.
- Policy must be documented and approved by Finance.

### Carpooling

- Only the driver may claim mileage (passengers do not claim).
- Pre-approval required for booking if claiming toll/parking.

### Public Transport

- Reimbursement: Actual fare based on receipt (Grab/ taxi, train, bus, LRT, MRT).
- Mileage rate does NOT apply when using public transport.
