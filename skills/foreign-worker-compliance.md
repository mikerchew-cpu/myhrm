# Malaysia Foreign Worker Compliance Guide

**Department:** HR

---

## Overview

Malaysia has a comprehensive regulatory framework governing the employment of foreign workers. This guide covers work permits, levies, medical examinations, visa management, statutory contributions, housing, termination, and record-keeping requirements.

---

## Work Permits — Visit Pass (Temporary Employment)

The primary work permit for foreign workers in Malaysia is the **Visit Pass (Temporary Employment)** — also known as **PLKS (Pas Lawatan Kerja Sementara)** .

### Eligibility Criteria

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
 | Nationality | Approved source countries only (varies by sector; includes Indonesia, Nepal, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Vietnam, Philippines, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Cambodia, Laos, India, and others per government policy) |
| Age | Typically 18–45 years (varies by sector) |
| Health | Must pass FOMEMA medical examination |
| Sector | Quota-based — manufacturing, construction, services, plantation, agriculture, domestic help |
| Valid passport | Minimum 18 months validity |

### Quota Approval Process

```
Employer applies for quota via ePPAx / JTK portal
           ↓
Quota approved (or rejected with reason)
           ↓
Employer submits visa application (Calling Visa / Visa with Reference)
           ↓
Worker enters Malaysia (VDRL / social visit pass)
           ↓
Worker undergoes FOMEMA medical check-up
           ↓
Worker registers for biometrics (i-Kad issuance)
           ↓
PLKS issued (typically 1–5 years validity)
```

### Key Points

- Quota is **sector-specific** (cannot transfer between sectors).
- Quota approval does not guarantee levy approval.
- PLKS is tied to a **specific employer** (not transferable).
- Renewal must be applied **before expiry**.
- Each foreign worker must possess an **i-Kad** (foreign worker identification card).

## Levy — Multi-Tier Levy Mechanism (MTLM)

### Overview

The **Multi-Tier Levy Mechanism (MTLM)** replaced the flat levy rate system. Levy rates vary by:
- **Sector** (manufacturing, construction, services, plantation, agriculture)
- **Worker nationality** / source country tier
- **Skill level** (skilled vs semi-skilled)

### Indicative Levy Rates (Monthly Per Worker)

| Sector | Tier 1 (RM) | Tier 2 (RM) | Tier 3 (RM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing | 1,850 | 2,500 | 3,500 |
| Construction | 1,850 | 2,500 | 3,500 |
| Services | 1,850 | 2,500 | 3,500 |
| Plantation | 1,040 | 1,500 | 2,000 |
| Agriculture | 1,040 | 1,500 | 2,000 |

- Levy is paid **annually in advance** at permit renewal.
- Employers are responsible for the full levy amount.
- Levy deduction from worker's salary is **prohibited** (except for specific government-approved schemes).

### Source Country Tiers

- **Tier 1** (higher levy): Countries with higher documented compliance risk
- **Tier 2** (medium levy): Standard source countries
- **Tier 3** (lower levy): ASEAN neighbouring countries

Note: Exact rates are updated periodically by the Ministry of Home Affairs (KDN).

## FOMEMA — Medical Check-Up

### Requirements

- **Pre-entry:** Medical screening before issuance of PLKS.
- **Annual renewal:** Annual medical check-up mandatory.
- **Overdue status:** If medical check-up is overdue, permit renewal is **blocked** until completed.

### Medical Tests

| Test | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Chest X-ray | Tuberculosis screening |
| Blood test | HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B |
| Urine test | Drug screening |
| Physical examination | General health assessment |
| Pregnancy test | For female workers |

### Outcome

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| Fit | Continue permit process |
| Unfit (treatable) | Treatment required; re-test after recovery |
| Unfit (permanent/ infectious) | Permit denied; worker repatriated |

## Visa Expiry & Overstay

### Renewal Timeline

- Permit renewal must be initiated **at least 1–3 months before expiry**.
- Late renewal may require pay of a late-payment penalty.
- If the permit has expired, renewal may be declined and the worker must exit and re-apply.

### Overstay Consequences

| Duration | Penalty |
|---|---|
| ≤ 30 days | Fines (RM500 per day of overstay, compounded) |
| 30–90 days | Higher fines + potential deportation |
| > 90 days | Deportation + blacklist from re-entry |

- Employer is responsible for ensuring the worker's pass is valid at all times.
- Workers found without valid passes may be detained at immigration depots.

## Security Bond

- **Required for certain sectors** (e.g., domestic workers, construction).
- Amount varies by sector and nationality:
  - Typically **RM 5,000 – RM 15,000** per worker.
- The bond is forfeited if the worker absconds, overstays, or breaches permit conditions.
- The bond is released when:
  - Worker returns to home country upon expiry of permit, or
  - Permit is transferred (if allowed), or
  - Worker becomes a permanent resident.

## Termination of Foreign Workers

### Employer-Initiated Termination

- Notify the **Director General of Immigration** within **30 days** of termination.
- Submit termination report via the ePPAx portal.
- Cancel the worker's PLKS.
- Arrange for repatriation (employer bears cost).
- The levy paid for the remaining permit period is generally **not refunded**.

### Worker Absconds (Disappears)

- Employer must report to Immigration within **14 days** of the date the worker is discovered missing.
- Also lodge a police report.
- Failure to report may result in the employer being held responsible for overstay.

### Voluntary Repatriation

- If the worker wishes to return home before permit expiry:
  - Employer must arrange and bear repatriation cost.
  - Submit final clearance to immigration.
  - Worker must exit within the prescribed timeframe.

## Minimum Wage

| Rate | Effective Date |
|---|---|
| **RM 1,700 per month** | February 2025 (5+ employees) |
| **RM 1,700 per month** | August 2025 (<5 employees) |

- Applies to **all foreign workers** as well as local workers.
- The wage must be paid in full; levy must **not** be deducted from wages.
- Daily-rated foreign workers: RM 65.38/day (based on 26 working days).

## Housing & Accommodation

### Legal Requirements

- **Workers' Minimum Standards of Housing and Amenities Act 1990 (Act 446)**.
- Employer must provide **adequate accommodation** that meets minimum standards.

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Minimum floor area per worker | 4.5 m² |
| Ventilation | Natural or mechanical ventilation |
| Sanitary facilities | 1 toilet per 15 workers |
| Water supply | Adequate and clean |
| Kitchen facilities | Provided (or meal arrangements) |
| Electricity | Adequate supply |

### Penalty for Non-Compliance

- Fines up to **RM 50,000** per offence.
- Failure to register accommodation with JTK (Department of Labour) is an offence.

## EPF for Foreign Workers

| Effective Date | Requirement | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Until September 2025 | Voluntary | Negotiable between employer and worker |
| **From October 2025** | **Mandatory** | **2% employee + 2% employer** |

- Wage ceiling: RM 5,000 per month.
- Must be registered through the EPF i-Akaun portal.
- Late payment penalties apply as per standard EPF rules.

---

## SOCSO for Foreign Workers

- **SOCSO Category 4** (Employment Injury + Invalidity Pension) coverage.
- **Employer only** contribution (no employee share required).
- Rates follow the same table as Category 1 employer portion.
- Wage ceiling: RM 6,000 per month.

| Monthly Wage (RM) | Employer Contribution (RM) |
|---|---|
| ≤ 30.00 | 1.30 |
| 30.01 – 50.00 | 2.15 |
| ... | ... |
| ≥ 4,000.01 | 596.35 |

- Registration required within 30 days of hiring.
- Late payment: Penalty up to 30%.

---

## EIS for Foreign Workers

- **Does NOT apply** to foreign workers.
- EIS (Employment Insurance System) is for Malaysian citizens and PRs only.

---

## HRDF for Foreign Workers

- Employers with 10+ employees must pay HRDF levy at **1% of monthly wages** (for Malaysian employees only).
- Foreign workers are **excluded** from HRDF contribution.
- However, foreign workers may attend HRDF-funded training at the employer's request.

## Record Keeping

| Document | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Passport copy (bio page + latest pass) | Duration of employment + 6 years |
| PLKS / i-Kad copy | Duration of employment + 6 years |
| Levy payment receipts | 6 years |
| FOMEMA medical reports | 6 years |
| Security bond documents | Until released |
| Employment contract | 6 years |
| Termination / repatriation records | 6 years |

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

| Offence | Maximum Penalty |
|---|---|
| Employing worker without valid permit | RM 10,000 per worker or jail |
| Levy not paid | RM 10,000 + revocation |
| FOMEMA overdue | Permit renewal blocked |
| Housing violation | Up to RM 50,000 per offence |
| EPF non-compliance | Up to RM 10,000 + imprisonment |
| SOCSO non-registration | Up to RM 10,000 + imprisonment |
