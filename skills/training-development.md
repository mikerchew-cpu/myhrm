# Training & Development — HRD Corp Guide

**Department:** HR

---

## HRD Corp (formerly HRDF)

HRD Corp is a statutory agency under the Ministry of Human Resources regulating workplace training in Malaysia.

### Registration Requirements

| Company Category | Registration |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Manufacturing | Mandatory if 10+ Malaysian employees |
| Services | Mandatory if 10+ Malaysian employees |
| Mining & Quarrying | Mandatory if 10+ Malaysian employees |
| Any sector (voluntary) | Optional if 5–9 employees |

*Applies to companies registered under the Companies Act 2016.*

---

## Levy Rate

| Category | Contribution Rate | Based On |
|----------|------------------|----------|
| Mandatory (10+ employees) | 1.0% of monthly wages | Basic + fixed allowances |
| Voluntary levy (5–9 employees) | 0.5% of monthly wages | Basic + fixed allowances |

**Levy Cap:** Levied on monthly wages up to RM20,000 per employee.

---

## Grant Claims — e-TRiS System

- **Platform:** e-TRiS (HRD Corp's online portal)
- **Claim Limit:** Up to the total levy amount paid (accumulated)
- **Eligibility:** Training must be conducted by an HRD Corp registered/certified trainer or training provider
- **Claim Period:** Submit within 90 days of training completion
- **Disbursement:** Approved claims reimbursed within 30 working days

### Types of Claims

| Claim Type | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Course fees | Training programme fees inclusive of materials |
| Trainer fees | External trainer costs for in-house programmes |
| Certification | Professional certification exam fees |
| e-Learning | Approved online course platforms |
| OJT (limited) | Structured on-the-job training (pre-approved) |

---

## Eligible Training

- Skills-based courses
- Professional certifications (e.g., HR, Finance, IT)
- Leadership and management programmes
- Technical and vocational training
- Compliance and regulatory training
- E-learning modules on accredited platforms
- Seminars, workshops, conferences (industry-related)

**Not eligible:**
- In-house meetings
- Induction/orientation
- General company briefings
- Team-building activities without learning outcomes

## Training Needs Analysis (TNA)

TNA is the annual process of identifying skill gaps and planning training interventions.

**TNA Process:**
1. **Align** — Map training needs to business goals and KPI gaps
2. **Survey** — Collect input from department heads and employees
3. **Identify** — Pinpoint skill gaps via performance reviews, assessments, competency frameworks
4. **Prioritise** — Rank by urgency, budget, and strategic impact
5. **Plan** — Create annual training plan with budget allocation

**Output:** Annual Training Plan (ATP) with:
- Course names
- Target participants
- Estimated cost
- Delivery mode
- Expected outcomes

## Types of Training

| Type | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Internal (In-House) | Conducted by internal trainer or external trainer onsite | Company policy training, software training |
| External (Public) | Open enrolment courses at training centres | Leadership workshop, ISO auditor course |
| e-Learning | Self-paced online modules | LinkedIn Learning, HRD Corp e-Learning |
| Certification | Exam-based professional qualification | CHRP, ACCA, PMP, CISSP |
| Blended | Mix of online and classroom | Management certification programme |
| On-the-Job (OJT) | Structured mentoring with assessment (requires pre-approval) | Apprenticeship, job rotation |

## Record Keeping

| Record | Retention |
|--------|-----------|
| Certificate of completion | 6 years |
| Attendance sheet | 6 years |
| Training evaluation forms | 3 years |
| Training Needs Analysis (TNA) | 6 years |
| Annual Training Plan | 6 years |
| Grant claim documents | 7 years |

Records must be available for HRD Corp audit on request.

---

## Annual Report Submission

| Item | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Annual Training Plan | Submit by 31 January (or within 30 days of financial year) |
| Actual Training Spend Report | Submit within 90 days of financial year-end |
| Grant Adjustment | Based on actual vs planned spend |

**Consequence:** Failure to submit may result in levy suspension or penalty.

## Grants & Incentives

### Double Deduction Tax Incentive

| Condition | Benefit |
|-----------|---------|
| Approved training under HRD Corp levy | Double deduction on training expenditure |
| Training for employees with salary ≤ RM12,000/month | Additional incentive available |
| Courses must be accredited | Tax deduction only for approved courses |

### HRD Corp Grant Programmes

- **Grant 1.0** — Standard grant for all levy-paying employers
- **Grant 2.0** — Special grants for SMEs, digital upskilling, and B40 training
- **B40 Grant** — Training for lower-income workers
- **Penjana HRDF** — COVID recovery reskilling/upskilling
- **Industry-Specific Grants** — Targeted grants for sectors in transition

---

## HRD Corp Levy Payment

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Due Date | 15th of the following month |
| Late Payment Penalty | 15% per annum on outstanding amount |
| Submission | Monthly via e-TRiS or HRD Corp portal |
| Levy Statement | Downloadable from e-TRiS for reconciliation |

## Key Reminders

- Register with HRD Corp before hiring threshold is triggered — backdated levy may apply
- Keep training provider details updated in e-TRiS
- Pre-approval required for grant claims — do not claim without prior approval
- Retain participant attendance evidence for audit
- Use HRD Corp's accredited course directory to verify training providers
