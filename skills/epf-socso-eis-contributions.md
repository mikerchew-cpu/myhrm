# Malaysia EPF, SOCSO, EIS Contribution Guide

**Department:** HR

---

## Overview

Employers in Malaysia are legally required to register and contribute to several statutory funds for their employees. This guide covers the mandatory contributions for EPF (KWSP), SOCSO (PERKESO), EIS (SIP), PCB (MTD), and HRDF, including rates, deadlines, and compliance requirements.

---

## EPF (KWSP) — Employees Provident Fund

### Who Must Contribute

- **Mandatory:** Malaysian citizens and Permanent Residents
- **Foreign workers:** Previously voluntary; **mandatory at 2% each (employer & employee)** effective **October 2025**
- **Domestic servants:** Optional registration under specific scheme
- **Excluded:** Non-Malaysian citizens working less than 30 days (or as exempted)

### Contribution Rates — Citizens & PRs

| Monthly Salary | Employee Share | Employer Share | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ RM5,000 | 11% | 13% | 24% |
| > RM5,000 | 11% | 12% | 23% |

### Contribution Rates — Age 60 & Above

| Category | Employee Share | Employer Share |
|---|---|---|
| Citizens / PRs | 0% (optional up to 5%) | 4% |
| Foreign workers | Exempt (separate rules) | Exempt |

### Contribution Rates — Foreign Workers (from Oct 2025)

| Party | Rate |
|---|---|
| Employee | 2% |
| Employer | 2% |

### Key Rules

- Contributions are calculated on **monthly wages** including basic salary, fixed allowances, commission, and OT (subject to wage ceiling where applicable).
- **Bonus** and variable payments: contributions deducted at 11% employee / 12–13% employer.
- **Wage ceiling** for EPF: RM20,000 per month (no contribution on wages above RM20,000). However, for non-citizens, the ceiling is RM5,000 per month (from Oct 2025).
- **Due date:** 15th of the following month.
- Late payment penalty: charged at a rate determined by KWSP (currently up to 12% per annum).

### Categories

| Category | Coverage | Who Can Join |
|---|---|---|
| **Category 1** | Employment Injury + Invalidity Pension | Employees under 60 |
| **Category 2** | Employment Injury only | Employees aged 60+ |
| **Category 3** | Employment Injury only | Domestic servants |

### Key Rules

- **Wage ceiling:** RM **6,000** per month (effective October 2024).
- **Due date:** 15th of the following month.
- Contributions must be reported and paid through the **ASSIST Portal** or authorised banks.
- **Late payment:** Penalty up to 30% (varies by duration of default).
- First-time registration: Employer must register with PERKESO within 30 days of hiring.

## EIS (SIP) — Employment Insurance System

### Contribution Rates

| Component | Rate |
|---|---|
| Employer | 0.2% of monthly wages |
| Employee | 0.2% of monthly wages |
| **Total** | **0.4% of monthly wages** |

### Who Must Contribute

- **Malaysian citizens** and **Permanent Residents**
- **Excludes** foreign workers
- Applies from first day of employment

### Contribution Range

| Monthly Wage (RM) | Total Contribution (RM) |
|---|---|
| ≤ 30.00 | 0.10 |
| 30.01 – 6,000.00 | 0.4% of wage |
| > 6,000.00 | Capped at RM24.00 (0.4% × 6,000) |

### Key Rules

- **Wage ceiling:** RM **6,000** per month.
- **Due date:** 15th of the following month.
- EIS provides job search allowance, training, early re-employment allowance, and income disruption allowance.
- Administered by PERKESO (SIP portal).

## PCB (MTD) — Monthly Tax Deduction

### Rates (Progressive)

| Chargeable Income Bracket (RM) | Rate (%) | Tax (RM) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 5,000 | 0% | 0 |
| 5,001 – 20,000 | 1% | 0 – 150 |
| 20,001 – 35,000 | 3% | 150 – 600 |
| 35,001 – 50,000 | 8% | 600 – 1,800 |
| 50,001 – 70,000 | 14% | 1,800 – 4,600 |
| 70,001 – 100,000 | 21% | 4,600 – 10,900 |
| 100,001 – 250,000 | 25% | 10,900 – 48,400 |
| 250,001 – 400,000 | 25.5% | 48,400 – 86,650 |
| 400,001 – 600,000 | 26% | 86,650 – 138,650 |
| 600,001 – 1,000,000 | 28% | 138,650 – 250,650 |
| 1,000,001 – 2,000,000 | 30% | 250,650 – 550,650 |
| > 2,000,000 | 33% | 550,650 + |

### Key Rules

- Calculated using LHDN's PCB formula (monthly or bi-monthly).
- Employer deducts at source each month.
- **Due date:** 15th of the following month.
- Annual reconciliation via Form E (employer) and BE/B (employee).

## HRDF (PSMB) — Human Resource Development Fund

### Contribution Rates

| Employer Size (Malaysian Employees) | Rate |
|---|---|
| 10+ employees (manufacturing) | 1% of monthly wages |
| 10+ employees (services, etc.) | 1% of monthly wages |
| 5–9 employees (voluntary) | 0.5% of monthly wages |

### Key Rules

- Mandatory for employers with **10 or more** Malaysian employees in covered sectors.
- Levy is capped at a monthly wage ceiling (revised periodically; currently up to RM10,000 wage base for contribution purposes).
- Registered employers can claim training grants.
- **Due date:** 15th of the following month.
- Late payment penalty: 10% per annum.

## Minimum Wage

| Effective Date | Rate | Applicable To |
|---|---|---|
| **February 2025** | **RM 1,700 / month** | Employers with 5+ employees |
| **August 2025** | **RM 1,700 / month** | Employers with < 5 employees |

- Applies to **all workers** including foreign workers.
- Excludes domestic servants (separate minimum wage order applies).
- Daily equivalent: RM 65.38 (based on 26 working days).
- Hourly equivalent: RM 8.17 (based on 8-hour day).

## Important Notes

1. All contributions must be remitted via the respective online portals (EPF i-Akaun, PERKESO ASSIST, HRDCorp eTRiS, LHDN ePCB).
2. Quarterly or annual forms: EPF Form A, SOCSO Contribution Statement, EIS Return, PCB Annual Return (Form E).
3. Failure to register or pay contributions is a criminal offence and may result in fines, imprisonment, or both.
4. Wage ceiling changes occur periodically — always verify current rates.
