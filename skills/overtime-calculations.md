# Malaysia Overtime Calculation Guide

**Department:** HR

---

## Overview

This guide covers the calculation and payment of overtime (OT) under the Malaysia Employment Act 1955, including eligibility, rates, worked examples, legal limits, and record-keeping requirements.

---

## Eligibility for Overtime Pay

Under Section 60(3) of the Employment Act 1955, overtime applies to:

| Category | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Wage-based** | Employees earning **≤ RM4,000 per month** |
| **Manual labour** | Employees engaged in manual labour **regardless of salary** |
| **Excluded** | Employees earning > RM4,000/month in non-manual roles (unless contract provides OT) |

**Note:** Some employees earning > RM4,000 may still be entitled to OT under their employment contract or collective agreement — always check the contract.

---

## Key Definitions

| Term | Formula | Example (Salary RM3,900) |
|---|---|---|
| **ORP** (Ordinary Rate of Pay) | Monthly Salary ÷ 26 | RM3,900 ÷ 26 = **RM150.00/day** |
| **HRP** (Hourly Rate of Pay) | ORP ÷ Normal Daily Hours | RM150 ÷ 8 = **RM18.75/hour** |

**Normal daily hours:** Usually 8 hours (per Employment Act max). If the contract provides fewer normal hours, use that figure.

---

## Overtime Rates Summary

| Situation | Rate | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal working day** (beyond normal hours) | **1.5 × HRP** | HRP × 1.5 × OT hours |
| **Rest day** — worked ≤ half normal hours | **0.5 × ORP** | ORP × 0.5 |
| **Rest day** — worked > half normal hours | **1.0 × ORP** | ORP × 1.0 |
| **Rest day** — worked exceeding normal hours (extra beyond daily norm) | **2.0 × HRP** | HRP × 2.0 × extra hours |
| **Public holiday** — worked within normal hours | **2.0 × ORP** | ORP × 2.0 × normal hours |
| **Public holiday** — worked beyond normal hours | **3.0 × HRP** | HRP × 3.0 × OT hours |

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Normal Day OT

**Scenario:** Employee earns RM3,900/month. Works 2 hours OT on a Tuesday.

| Step | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| ORP | RM3,900 ÷ 26 | RM150.00 |
| HRP | RM150.00 ÷ 8 | RM18.75 |
| OT hours | 2 hours | 2 |
| OT rate | 1.5 × RM18.75 | RM28.125/hr |
| **Total OT pay** | 2 × RM28.125 | **RM56.25** |

---

### Example 2: Rest Day OT (≤ Half Normal Hours)

**Scenario:** Same employee works 3 hours on a Sunday (rest day). Normal hours = 8.

3 hours is ≤ 4 hours (½ of 8), so the rate is 0.5 × ORP.

| Step | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| ORP | RM3,900 ÷ 26 | RM150.00 |
| 0.5 × ORP | 0.5 × RM150.00 | RM75.00 |
| **Total OT pay** | — | **RM75.00** |

---

### Example 3: Rest Day OT (> Half Normal Hours, Within Normal Hours)

**Scenario:** Employee works 6 hours on a Sunday (rest day). Normal hours = 8.

6 hours > 4 hours (½ of 8). So payment is 1.0 × ORP for the first 8 hours.

| Step | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| ORP | RM3,900 ÷ 26 | RM150.00 |
| 1.0 × ORP | 1.0 × RM150.00 | RM150.00 |
| **Total OT pay** | — | **RM150.00** |

---

### Example 4: Rest Day OT (Exceeding Normal Hours)

**Scenario:** Employee works 10 hours on a Sunday (rest day). Normal hours = 8.

First 8 hours: 1.0 × ORP  
Additional 2 hours: 2.0 × HRP

| Step | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| ORP | RM3,900 ÷ 26 | RM150.00 |
| HRP | RM150.00 ÷ 8 | RM18.75 |
| First 8 hours pay | 1.0 × RM150.00 | RM150.00 |
| Extra 2 hours pay | 2.0 × RM18.75 × 2 | RM75.00 |
| **Total OT pay** | RM150.00 + RM75.00 | **RM225.00** |

---

### Example 5: Public Holiday Work (Normal Hours Only)

**Scenario:** Employee works 8 hours on National Day (public holiday).

| Step | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| ORP | RM3,900 ÷ 26 | RM150.00 |
| 2.0 × ORP | 2.0 × RM150.00 | RM300.00 |
| **Total OT pay** | — | **RM300.00** |

---

### Example 6: Public Holiday Work (With OT Beyond Normal Hours)

**Scenario:** Employee works 10 hours on a public holiday. Normal hours = 8.

First 8 hours: 2.0 × ORP  
Additional 2 hours: 3.0 × HRP

| Step | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| ORP | RM3,900 ÷ 26 | RM150.00 |
| HRP | RM150.00 ÷ 8 | RM18.75 |
| First 8 hours pay | 2.0 × RM150.00 | RM300.00 |
| Extra 2 hours pay | 3.0 × RM18.75 × 2 | RM112.50 |
| **Total OT pay** | RM300.00 + RM112.50 | **RM412.50** |

## Overtime Limits

### Monthly Limit

| Provision | Limit |
|---|---|
| Standard OT per month | **104 hours** (maximum without approval) |
| Beyond 104 hours | Requires **Director General's approval** |

### Daily Limit

| Provision | Limit |
|---|---|
| Maximum total work hours (normal + OT) | **12 hours per day** |
| Maximum spread-over period | **10 hours per day** |

**Important:** These limits apply per employee. Employers cannot force OT beyond these limits without proper authorisation.

---

## OT Calculation for Shift Workers

- For shift workers, the normal working hours per day are as stated in the employment contract (typically 8 hours per shift).
- If a shift spans midnight, all hours worked are attributed to the day the shift commenced unless otherwise agreed.
- Shift differentials (e.g., night shift allowance) are separate from OT — the OT rate is applied to the base HRP/ORP.

---

## OT for Part-Time Employees

- Pro-rate the normal daily hours based on agreed schedule.
- OT kicks in only when hours exceed the agreed normal daily hours (not the standard 8).
- Use the same ORP/HRP formulas based on actual salary.

---

## OT for Employees on Fixed Salary > RM4,000

- OT entitlement is not guaranteed under the Act.
- Many employers include OT in the contractual package (e.g., "OT inclusive" or "all-in salary").
- If the contract is silent, the employee is not entitled to OT under the Act but may negotiate.

## Record-Keeping Requirements

| Requirement | Period |
|---|---|
| Maintain OT records (hours worked, pay, approvals) | **6 years** |
| Itemised payslip showing OT separately | Mandatory since 1 Jan 2023 |

### Records Must Include:
- Date and time OT started/ended
- Number of OT hours
- Rate applied
- Total OT paid
- Employee acknowledgement (recommended)

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---|---|
| Using 30 days instead of 26 for ORP | ORP = Salary ÷ **26** (not 30) |
| Using flat HRP for PH OT | PH OT = **3.0 × HRP** (not 1.5) |
| Paying ORP × 0.5 for any rest day work | Only if ≤ ½ normal hours; > ½ normal hours = 1.0 × ORP |
| Not capping OT at 12 hrs/day | Employment Act absolute limit is 12 hrs/day (normal + OT) |
| Forgetting to maintain records | Records must be kept for 6 years |
